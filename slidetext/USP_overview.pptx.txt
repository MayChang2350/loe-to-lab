[NOTES] Yield locus（屈服軌跡／屈服曲線）: A graph showing the combinations of normal stress and shear stress at which the powder just begins to flow or fail.
Angle of internal friction（內摩擦角）: Measures the frictional resistance between powder particles. A larger angle generally indicates greater resistance to sliding and poorer flow.
Unconfined yield strength, fcf​（無側限屈服強度）: The compressive stress required to make a previously consolidated powder fail when it is no longer laterally confined. A higher value means the powder is more resistant to flow and more likely to form stable lumps or arches.
Powder cohesion（粉體凝聚力／粉體內聚力）: The attractive strength holding powder particles together when normal stress is very low or zero. Higher cohesion usually means a stickier, less free-flowing powder.
Flow function coefficient, ffc（流動函數係數）: A numerical indicator of powder flowability, commonly calculated as ffc, A higher ffc indicates better powder flow, while a lower value indicates a more cohesive powder.
