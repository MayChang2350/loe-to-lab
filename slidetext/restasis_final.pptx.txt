--- slide 3 ---
NDA 050790 的法規與產品定位
Product Snapshot
Proprietary name: RESTASIS / RESTASIS MULTIDOSE
Active ingredient: Cyclosporine
NDA: 050790
Dosage form / route: Emulsion；ophthalmic
Strength: 0.05% = 0.5 mg/mL
 Therapeutic class: calcineurin inhibitor immunosuppressant
Use: increases tear production in KCS-associated ocular inflammation
Clinical Label Core
Indication: tear production presumed suppressed due to ocular inflammation associated with keratoconjunctivitis sicca.
Limitation: increased tear production was not seen in patients using topical anti-inflammatory drugs or punctal plugs.
Dose: one drop twice daily, approximately 12 hours apart.
Safety: most common adverse event historically ocular burning.
Development Implication
Locally acting ophthalmic complex emulsion：systemic PK is not the primary equivalence bridge.
Formulation performance depends on droplet / globule distribution, rheology, phase distribution and product-device interface.
Sterile preservative-free packaging raises container-closure and microbial control burden.

--- slide 5 ---
Label 不只是臨床資訊，也是處方／製程的線索
Description / formulation clues
• White opaque/slightly translucent homogeneous emulsion.
• Active：cyclosporine 0.05%.
• Inactives：glycerin、castor oil、polysorbate 80、carbomer copolymer type A、purified water、sodium hydroxide to adjust pH.
• pH：6.5–8.0; Osmolality：230–320 mOsmol/kg.
Use / packaging clues
• Single-use vial：invert before use, discard immediately after use.
• MultiDose：prime before first use; preservative-free multi-dose presentation.
• Contact lenses：remove before dosing; reinsert after 15 minutes.
• Storage: controlled room temperature in label versions.
R&D implications
• Q1/Q2 target likely constrained by PSG.
• Emulsion appearance and phase behavior become release/stability signals.
• Ophthalmic sterility + preservative-free format increases aseptic process and container closure validation requirements.
• Patient handling matters: dropper/vial UI may need comparative analysis.

Generic approvals
Known approved ANDA sponsors include Mylan, Teva, Deva/Devatis, Saptalis, Amneal, TWi, Jiangsu Anbison, and Bora/Upsher-Smith launch.

TE：AB code indicates FDA considers products therapeutically equivalent to the listed reference product when conditions match.
What to export

Interpretation risk
TE code is product-specific and does not mean all cyclosporine ophthalmic products are interchangeable. Different strengths, formulations, dosage forms, or presentations may have different reference standards and study requirements.
